= Dryland training

The Dryland training screen lets you record and review out-of-water workouts — strength, conditioning, and other gym-based sessions — alongside your swim history.

== Reaching this page

Navigate to *Dryland Workouts* via the bottom navigation bar or rail (route `/dryland`).
The screen opens with a chronological list of your logged workouts.
When no workouts have been recorded yet, a prompt invites you to add your first entry.

== Common actions

=== Log a new workout

Tap *New Workout* (the floating action button at the bottom of the screen) to open the workout entry sheet.
Fill in the date, an optional notes field, and at least one exercise, then tap *Save*.

=== Edit an existing workout

Open the overflow menu (three-dot icon) on any workout card and choose *Edit*.
The same entry sheet opens pre-filled with the existing values.
Change any fields and tap *Save* to update the record.

=== Delete a workout

Open the overflow menu on a workout card and choose *Delete*.
A confirmation dialogue asks you to confirm before the workout and all its exercises are removed.

=== Save a workout as a routine template

Open the overflow menu on any workout card and choose *Save as routine*.
Enter a name in the dialogue and tap *Save*.
The workout's exercises are stored as a reusable template.

=== Use a routine template

Tap the folder icon in the top-right corner of the screen to open the *Routine Templates* sheet.
Each saved template shows its name and exercise count.
Tap *Use routine* to create a new workout dated today, pre-filled with the template's exercises.
To remove a template you no longer need, tap the delete icon next to it.

== Important fields

The following fields are recorded for each workout and its exercises.

=== Workout fields

`Date`::
The date the session took place.
Defaults to today; tap the date row in the entry sheet to pick a different date.
Dates before 2000 and future dates cannot be selected.

`Notes` (optional)::
Free-text remarks about the session — intensity, conditions, or anything else worth recording.
Displayed below the exercise list when a workout card is expanded.

=== Exercise fields

Each workout must contain at least one exercise.
Exercises are added individually within the workout entry sheet.

`Exercise name`::
A short description of the movement (for example, _Pull-ups_ or _Squat_).
This field is required.

`Sets`::
The number of sets performed.
Must be a positive integer.
Defaults to `3`.

`Reps`::
The number of repetitions per set.
Must be a positive integer.
Defaults to `10`.

`Weight (kg)` (optional)::
The load used, in kilograms.
Leave blank for bodyweight exercises.
Decimal values are accepted (for example, `22.5`).

== Expected behaviour

On *Save*, the workout and all its exercises are written to the local SQLite database and the list on screen refreshes immediately.
No network connection is required.

On *Delete* (confirmed), the workout record and every exercise belonging to it are removed from the database.
A brief snackbar confirms the deletion.

[NOTE]
====
Dryland workouts are queued for future synchronisation in the background.
The queue operates silently; a failure to enqueue does not prevent the local save from completing.
====
